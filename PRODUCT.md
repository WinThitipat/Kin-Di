# KIN DI
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Users
Thai-speaking people deciding what to eat or what to cook with ingredients at home.
## Product Purpose
Make choosing a meal easier through recipe roulette and ingredient matching.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Preserve roulette, fridge matching, community recipe sharing, favorites, dietary restrictions, existing recipe data and Supabase integration. Next.js 15, React 19, TypeScript and Tailwind 4.
## Brand Commitments
KIN DI / กินดิ! Thai-first interface. User authorizes a complete visual redesign and delegates creative direction.
## Evidence on Hand
Existing routes, seed recipes, ingredient catalog and safety engine in src. No verified testimonials or usage statistics.
